    Before you go

    • Check your passport now. For Croatia it needs to be issued less than 10 years before we land, and valid for 3 months after we fly home. Renewals can take weeks. The gov.uk rules
    • Get a GHIC. It's free on the NHS website, so ignore any site that tries to charge you.
    • Sort travel insurance once flights are booked.
    • New EU border checks. You might have fingerprints and a photo taken at passport control. It's free, just a bit more queueing.

    Croatia 101

    EurosCards work most places. Carry a bit of cash for boats and beach bars.
